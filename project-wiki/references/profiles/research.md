# Research profile

Recover the user's research questions and scope. Suggested types: topic, paper, method, experiment, concept. Sources may include local papers converted to text, reading notes and experiment reports. Record original publication/URL when available.

Organize around questions, methods and evidence rather than one summary per file. Distinguish reported results, independently reproduced results, hypotheses and disagreements. Record conditions and limitations of experiments. Publication recency alone does not supersede an earlier result.
